Wikidata, Commons & SPARQL Integration

By querying the open Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons APIs, modern web applications can discover thousands of classic paintings and extract rich quantized palettes client-side without API keys.

"When structured Wikidata records meet client-side color quantization, static design tokens become dynamic museum-grade expressions."
